Assessment Guard

Your instructor and D2L are the source of truth. This tool does not connect to D2L, does not sync automatically, and does not send reminders. It only helps you record dates you have checked yourself and export them to your own calendar.

DEMO MODE — SYNTHETIC DATA. Nothing here is a real assessment, and nothing you do in demo mode is saved. Your own saved list is untouched.

How to use it (2 minutes)
  1. Open the official announcement for the assessment — D2L or your instructor's email. Keep it open next to this page.
  2. In step 1 below, choose the assessment type and type the date and time exactly as the announcement says. Check the grey line under the date: it shows the day and timezone that will be saved.
  3. Choose “Yes — I checked it myself”, pick where you checked it, and type the announcement title or email subject. Not sure yet? Choose “Not yet” — it will be flagged as unverified.
  4. Press Save assessment. It appears in step 2, in date order.
  5. On that assessment, press Add to Google Calendar or Add to Outlook (a new tab opens with the event filled in — press Save there), or Download .ics file for Apple Calendar and others.
  6. In your calendar, check the time and add reminders (we suggest 7 days, 48 hours and 24 hours before). Then come back and press “I checked it is in my calendar”.
  7. Open this page again whenever you like: anything within 48 hours, unverified, or not yet in your calendar is flagged at the top of step 2.

Just looking? Press Show synthetic demo at the top to see example assessments. Demo data is fake and never saved.

      4. Your data

      Everything is stored only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing browser data, using private browsing, or switching devices will lose it — download a backup.

      How it decides

      1. Input a date you copied from an official source, and say where you checked it.
      2. Validate: reject missing or impossible dates; “verified” needs a source.
      3. Store it in this browser only.
      4. Decide for each assessment: passed, within 24 hours, within 48 hours, within 7 days, or later — plus whether it is unverified or not yet confirmed in your calendar.
      5. Repeat the check every time you open the page.
      6. Act: export to your calendar, then confirm it arrived.

      No AI, no hidden decisions. This is a student learning project; it does not grant extensions, excuse missed assessments, or replace university systems.
